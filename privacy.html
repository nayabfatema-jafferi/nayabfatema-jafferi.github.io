Privacy policy

Threadline · last updated 15 November 2026

Threadline is a puzzle game. It collects as little as possible: no email, no phone number, no real name, no location, no ads and no advertising tracking.

What is stored online

Only if you choose to join the daily leaderboard:

  • A random player ID that the app creates for your device, so your scores stay yours. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • The username you pick. Other players can see it on the leaderboard.
  • Your time for each daily puzzle (Line and Knots) you solve, and when you submitted it. Other players can see your username and time on that day's leaderboard.

This is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which runs the leaderboard for us.

If you skip choosing a username, nothing is sent online.

What stays on your device

Your level progress and stars, streaks, friends list and puzzles in progress are saved in the app’s storage on your phone only.

Puzzles you share

A puzzle you create becomes a code holding only the puzzle, the name and title you typed in, and your play-test time. You decide whether and with whom to share it.

Deleting your data

To remove your username and scores from the leaderboard, email us with your username and we'll delete them. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

Children

Threadline doesn't ask for personal information. Usernames are public, so we ask players not to use their real name.
